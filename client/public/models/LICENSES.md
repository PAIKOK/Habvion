Every model here is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(CC BY 4.0). None is NonCommercial, ShareAlike or NoDerivatives. The originals were
fetched through the Objaverse index of Sketchfab uploads, and the licence was checked on each
model's metadata.

Changes common to all: optimised with `@gltf-transform/cli optimize --texture-size 1024 --no-compress`
(mesh welding, simplification, textures resized to at most 1024 px, original image formats kept; no Draco).
In the app each model is scaled uniformly to fit its catalog item's box.
